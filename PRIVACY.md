# Privacy Policy — Malisa

**Last updated:** June 18, 2026

Malisa - AI web site Translator ("Malisa", "the extension") translates web pages using AI services **you** configure. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

## Data we process

| Data | Where | When |
|------|-------|------|
| **Page text** | Sent to your chosen AI provider | Only when you click Translate |
| **API keys & settings** | `chrome.storage.local` on your device | When you save Settings |
| **Translation cache** | Local storage on your device | Optional (can disable or clear in Settings) |
| **Model lists** | Local cache (~6 h) | When you refresh models |

## What we do not do

- No analytics or telemetry
- No selling or sharing data for marketing
- No proprietary Malisa servers for translation

## Third parties

When you translate, text is sent to the API of the provider you choose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, MyMemory, etc.). Their privacy policies and terms apply. Speech (popup Listen buttons and selection-popup Speak buttons) likewise sends text to the chosen speech provider (Google Cloud TTS, Gemini TTS, ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Microsoft Azure, Amazon Polly, or a custom/self-hosted endpoint you configure); browser voices stay on your device.

Note: the Gemini and OpenRouter free tiers may use submitted content to improve their services — avoid sensitive text when using them.

## Your controls

- Disable translation cache in Settings
- Clear translation cache at any time
- Remove API keys with the clear (×) button and Save
