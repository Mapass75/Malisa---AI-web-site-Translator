# Privacy Policy — Malisa

**Last updated:** June 18, 2026

Malisa - AI web site Translator ("Malisa", "the extension") translates web pages using AI services **you** configure. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

## Data we process

| Data | Where | When |
|------|-------|------|
| **Page text** | Sent to your chosen AI provider | Only when you click Translate |
| **API keys & settings** | `chrome.storage.local` on your device | When you save Settings |
| **Translation cache** | Local storage on your device | Optional (can disable or clear in Settings) |
| **Model lists** | Local cache (~6 h) | When you refresh models |

## What we do not do

- No analytics or telemetry
- No selling or sharing data for marketing
- No proprietary Malisa servers for translation

## Third parties

When you translate, text is sent to the API of the provider you choose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.). Their privacy policies and terms apply.

## Your controls

- Disable translation cache in Settings
- Clear translation cache at any time
- Remove API keys with the clear (×) button and Save
