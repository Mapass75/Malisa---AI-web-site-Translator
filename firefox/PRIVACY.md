# Privacy Policy — Malisa

**Last updated:** June 18, 2026.

Malisa - AI web site Translator ("Malisa", "extension") translates web pages using AI services you configure. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

## Data we process

- **Page text** — extracted only when you click Translate. Sent to the AI provider you select.
- **API keys & settings** — stored locally in `chrome.storage.local` on your device. Never sent to our servers.
- **Translation cache** — stored locally for faster repeat translations.

## What we do not do

- No analytics or telemetry.
- No selling or sharing data with third parties for marketing.
- No proprietary backend servers for translation.

## Third parties (AI providers)

When you translate, text is sent to the API of the provider you choose. Their privacy policies and terms apply. You are responsible for API keys and costs.

## Extension permissions

- **activeTab** — access the current tab only when you use the extension.
- **storage** — save settings and cache locally.
- **host_permissions** — network calls only to configured AI provider APIs (and local Ollama).

## Retention & deletion

Uninstall the extension or clear its data in Chrome settings to remove stored keys and cache.

## Contact

Open an issue on the GitHub repository or contact the developer listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
