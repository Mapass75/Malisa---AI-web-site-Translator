Privacy Policy — Malisa

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

Malisa - AI web site Translator ("Malisa", "extension") translates web pages using AI services you configure. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Data we process

  • Page text — read only when you click Translate page (popup) or Translate page with Malisa (right-click menu). Sent to your chosen translation provider. While the page stays translated, text that loads later is also sent, until you click Restore original.
  • Selected and typed text — sent to your chosen translation provider when you translate a selection or use the popup text box.
  • Text read aloud — sent to your chosen speech provider when you click Listen or Speak. With Browser voices it stays on your device.
  • API keys & settings — stored locally in the extension's storage on your device, without extra encryption. Each key is sent only to the provider it belongs to. Never sent to the developer.
  • Translation cache — stored locally for faster repeat translations.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or telemetry.
  • No selling or sharing data with third parties for marketing.
  • No proprietary backend servers for translation.

Third parties (AI providers)

When you translate or listen, text is sent directly to the API of the translation or speech provider you choose. Their privacy policies and terms apply. You are responsible for API keys and costs.

Extension permissions

  • activeTab — access the current tab only when you use the extension.
  • storage — save settings and cache locally.
  • scripting — load Malisa's own content script into the current tab when you start a translation.
  • contextMenus — the right-click items Translate with Malisa and Translate page with Malisa.
  • host permissions — network calls only to the translation and speech provider APIs, and to Ollama or a self-hosted TTS server on your computer.

Retention & deletion

Uninstall the extension or clear its data in browser settings to remove stored keys and cache.

Contact

Open an issue on the GitHub repository or contact the developer listed on the store listing.
